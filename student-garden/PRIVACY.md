# Privacy

Student Garden keeps notes, tasks, study records, saved video positions, settings and media in private app storage. Planning, studying, cards and habits work offline. Videos are optional and require Internet access. There are no analytics, advertising SDKs, required accounts or cloud sync.

When you search or open a video, NewPipeExtractor contacts YouTube and its media servers directly. YouTube receives your IP address, search terms and requests needed for playback. Video and playlist thumbnails and channel avatars are requested from YouTube image servers, including i.ytimg.com and Google image CDNs. Frame captures and recorded video notes are saved only in private local storage and user-exported backups. Student Garden does not upload your notes, flashcards, courses, habits or backups. It does not sign in to a Google account. Search results are kept in memory; only lessons you explicitly save and their playback positions are stored as study records. Playback pauses when the app goes into the background, and the next video never starts automatically. Remote service access can be restricted or unavailable.

Microphone permission is requested only when recording audio for a flashcard or note. Recording stops when the app goes into the background. The live waveform uses microphone amplitude locally; audio is never sent to YouTube. Notes can include a video ID and timestamp, stored locally and included with audio attachments in exported backups. System file pickers grant access only to selected files. Notification permission supports focus completion, lesson reviews, task deadlines, agenda starts, due flashcards and scheduled habits. Exact-alarm access is optional; without it Android can batch alerts. Notifications use generic text without your note, task, card or habit content. Private receivers restore schedules after reboot, application updates and clock/time-zone changes. Deadline reminders use actual task and agenda times. Daily lesson, card and habit reminders are opt-in and use the chosen study time; missed daily sessions wait for the next study time rather than firing on app open. Deadline alerts older than two hours are suppressed. Category switches, a study time and a test notification are available in Settings. Reminder delivery history stays in private local preferences. Force-stopping the app pauses alarms until it is opened again.

The optional app lock uses Android device credentials. This is an access gate, not separate database encryption. Android system backup is disabled. Exported backup ZIP files are readable and should be stored privately. Import validates paths and sizes and merges new records without replacing existing records.

Uninstalling removes private app data. Export a backup first. Trash supports recovery of deleted records within the app. The Android app does not automatically transfer data from the separate web application.

Playback reuses a small, short-lived in-memory cache of extracted stream metadata. The app’s custom playback diagnostic entries contain numeric timings only; these entries contain no video titles, IDs, URLs, note text or recordings and are not uploaded.

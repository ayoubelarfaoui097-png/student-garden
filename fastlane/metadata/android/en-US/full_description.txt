Keep your study work together on your device.

• Choose a day in Agenda, set the session time, and give each study session its own color. Organize tasks and courses.
• Write rich notes with bold, italic, headings, lists and editable tables. Undo formatting and table changes.
• Study text, image and audio cards in separate decks. Record audio using your microphone with a visible recording timer and Stop control.
• Review due cards in a shuffled order. Cards without a written question hide the empty question area and center the remaining content.
• Reveal answers and choose a review interval: Again, Hard, Good, then 48 hours, 7 days, one month and Complete.
• Schedule lesson reviews after 48 hours, 7 days, then one calendar month, with local reminder notifications.
• Get local reminders for task deadlines, agenda starts, due flashcards and scheduled habits. Choose categories and a daily practice time, check permissions and send a test alert in Settings. Android power settings can delay reminders; force-stopping pauses them until reopening.
• Track colored habits with a weekly ledger, calendar and simple progress statistics.
• Run a custom focus timer with a completion sound and native notifications.
• Use English, Arabic, French or Spanish, and light or dark appearance.
• Export and restore local backups. Optionally use your Android screen lock to protect the app.

No required account, ads, analytics, Google Play Services or Internet permission. Your existing web workspace remains separate. Android backups use their own format; there is no automatic web sync.
